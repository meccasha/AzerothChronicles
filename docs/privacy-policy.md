# Privacy policy (template)

> Operators: adapt to your deployment and jurisdiction. The web app shows the same text at `/privacy`.

**What we store.** Your email, display name and a salted Argon2 password hash; characters you link or
declare; game events from your addon uploads and Battle.net syncs; content you create (manual events,
comments, reactions, screenshots re-encoded without metadata); Battle.net OAuth tokens, encrypted at rest.
We never request or store your Battle.net password.

**Who can see it.** Group content is visible only to that group's members. Groups are never public.
Characters you link are visible to members of groups you belong to.

**Your controls.** Export all of your data (Account → Export my data). Delete your account at any time;
your personal data is removed, and shared group events remain without your profile. Unlink Battle.net to
delete stored tokens. Revoke companion upload tokens at any time.

**Third parties.** When you link Battle.net we call Blizzard's official APIs on your behalf. This service
is not affiliated with Blizzard Entertainment.
